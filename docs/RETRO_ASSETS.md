# A&D Arcade generated artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The existing covers were preserved in `art-originals/retro-redesign/`. The supplied portraits were used only as identity references for the About cutout.

Design reference: https://leonardomoreira.com.br/ — cream paper windows, teal CRT desktop, and a wooden shelf of vintage PC game boxes.

## About portrait

Saved asset: `public/ad-about-cutout.png`

Use case: identity-preserve / compositing. Asset type: transparent paper photo cutout for the About page of A&D Arcade. Input 1 is the man's identity photograph; Input 2 is the woman's identity photograph. Create one editorial photographic collage showing BOTH exact people side by side, man on left woman on right, waist-up, keeping their recognizable faces, hair, skin tone and glasses faithful to supplied photos. Dress them in their existing black shirt and dark green outfit. Their hands rest on two beige vintage 1990s CRT desktop computers at bottom, as a nostalgic software-magazine portrait. Subtle warm film color, printed-paper grain, lightly worn white cut-paper border around entire silhouette, like a real magazine photo cutout. Wide composition, both faces similar scale, friendly natural expressions. Isolated on genuinely transparent background, no room background, no text, no watermarks, no other people. No cartoon, no vector.

## Game covers

The final prompts, character corrections and generated source paths are recorded in `retro-asset-manifest.json`. Hobo covers were corrected against the running game. Hollow Knight uses an architectural cover after the first character-focused images were rejected. Covers are optimized to 768 pixels wide for the site; full-resolution generated sources remain at the paths in the manifest.

The hands loader uses genuine terminal characters in `public/ad-ascii-hands.txt`, converted from the generated monochrome hands artwork. The amber/green A&D star badge uses a local script font and SVG for crisp scaling.
